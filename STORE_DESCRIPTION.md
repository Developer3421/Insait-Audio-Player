## Long Description
*(up to 10 000 characters)*

**Insait Audio Player** is a fast, no-nonsense MP3 player built for Windows 10 and Windows 11. It gives you everything you need to enjoy your local music collection — nothing more, nothing less.

### Playlist Management

Drag-and-drop or browse to add MP3 files to your queue. Reorder tracks, remove individual songs, or clear the entire playlist in one click. Your playlist is automatically saved when you close the app and restored the next time you open it, so you never lose your place.

### Album Art

When your MP3 files contain embedded artwork (ID3 APIC tags), Insait Audio Player displays the cover directly in the player. Files without embedded art show a clean music icon instead — no broken images, no missing thumbnails.

### Playback Controls

- **Play / Pause / Stop** with keyboard-friendly buttons
- **Seek** to any point in a track using the progress bar
- **Volume control** — adjustable from 0 % to 100 %
- **Playback speed** — slow down to 0.5× or speed up to 2.0× without losing audio quality
- **Shuffle** — randomize your playlist order
- **Repeat** — loop a single track or the whole playlist

### Resume Where You Left Off

The player remembers the track you were listening to and your exact position in it. Reopen the app and continue from the same spot automatically.

### Set as Default MP3 Player

One button registers Insait Audio Player for `.mp3` files and opens the Windows Default Apps settings so you can confirm the change — no hidden registry edits, fully transparent.

### File Association

Once set as the default, double-clicking any `.mp3` file in File Explorer opens it directly in Insait Audio Player and adds it to your playlist.

### Privacy First

Your playlist data — track names, artists, albums, and file paths — is stored locally in your own `%LocalAppData%` folder, encrypted at rest using AES. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud. No account required. No telemetry.

### Resilient Storage

The app uses a SQLite database to persist your playlist. If the database is ever corrupted (power loss, disk error), Insait Audio Player detects the problem automatically on the next launch, backs up the damaged file, and creates a fresh database — so the app always starts cleanly.

### Lightweight & Native

Built on the Windows native audio engine (WinMM / MCI), Insait Audio Player has a minimal footprint and starts in under a second. There are no bundled streaming services, no subscription fees, and no advertisements.
